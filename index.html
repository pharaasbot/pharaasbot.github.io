Platform

Hardware, payments, and operations — integrated.

Each unit is engineered for unattended operation in African retail conditions. One system from cabinet to reconciliation.

Robotic dispense

Multi-bay mechanism handles 60 to 220 SKUs per unit. Mean cycle time under 12 seconds.

Native payments

M-Pesa STK push as primary rail. Visa and Mastercard contactless as fallback. Hourly reconciliation.

Hardened cabinet

Steel construction, IP54, anti-vandal panels. Internal alarm and remote lockdown.

Power resilience

Four-hour battery hold-up handles grid outages without interrupting trade. Solar variant available.

Telemetry

GSM + Wi-Fi. Real-time inventory, sales, and unit-health feeds to operator dashboard and SMS.

Operator console

Web dashboard for inventory, pricing, sales, and uptime. Fleet-wide for multi-unit operators.

Regulatory track

Bringing automated pharmacy to Kenya, alongside the regulator.

Pharaas is engaging the Pharmacy and Poisons Board and the Ministry of Health on a regulatory sandbox pilot. The model follows the precedent set by Grab2Go in Estonia, operating commercially under the Accelerate Estonia framework since 2023.
