Retail automation

Automate your shop.

Pharaas is a robotic system that automates a retail shop. It combines robotics with remote-operations software so customers can order and pay for products while the owner operates and monitors the shop remotely.

Talk to usSee how it works

Introduction

Run your shop without being there all the time.

A shop needs to serve customers, process sales, retrieve products, manage stock, and keep operating throughout the day.

Pharaas automates this workflow so the owner can manage the retail operation remotely. The physical shop remains the business. Pharaas provides the technology that operates it.

How it works

From customer order to completed sale.

01

Order

Customers browse the available products and select what they want to buy.

02

Pay

Customers complete payment using a supported payment method, including mobile money such as M-Pesa or card payment.

03

Fulfil

The robotic system retrieves the selected products from the shop's inventory and prepares the order.

04

Collect

The customer receives the completed order through the shop's collection process.

The owner

Operate the shop remotely.

Pharaas gives the owner visibility into what is happening inside the shop.

Monitor sales, transactions, inventory, and system activity through the remote-operations platform. When something needs attention, the system provides the information needed to respond.

What Pharaas does

One system for the whole retail workflow.

Robotic fulfilment

The system retrieves products from the shop's inventory and fulfils customer orders.

Customer ordering

Customers can browse products and place orders directly through the retail system.

Payments

The purchase workflow supports payment methods including mobile money and cards.

Inventory

Product and sales information gives the owner visibility into stock and product movement.

Remote operations

The owner can monitor and manage relevant shop functions remotely.

System monitoring

Operational information helps identify situations that require attention.

Multiple outlets

Manage your outlets remotely.

Pharaas is designed for operators who want to manage automated retail outlets without being physically present at every location.

The operations platform provides visibility into sales, inventory, transactions, and system activity across deployments.
