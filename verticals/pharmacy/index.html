Pharmacy

Automate pharmacy retail.

Pharaas applies its retail automation platform to pharmacy environments, combining customer ordering, payment, robotic product handling, and remote operations.

The workflow can be configured around the responsibilities of the pharmacy operator, licensed professionals, and the requirements applicable to the deployment.

Talk to us about pharmacy

The use case

Automate the retail workflow while keeping professional responsibility where it belongs.

Pharmacy is a retail environment with responsibilities that extend beyond ordinary product sales.

Pharaas provides the technology for customer interaction, payment, product handling, and remote operations. The pharmacy operator and appropriate licensed professionals remain responsible for the professional decisions and requirements that apply to the pharmacy service.

Pharmacy workflow

A pharmacy workflow designed around the deployment.

01

Customer request

The customer identifies the product or service they require.

02

Verification and review

The workflow can incorporate the checks and professional review required for the relevant transaction.

03

Authorisation

Where professional approval is required, the transaction proceeds according to the configured pharmacy workflow.

04

Robotic fulfilment

The system retrieves the approved product for the customer.

05

Record

Relevant transaction and operational information is maintained through the system.
