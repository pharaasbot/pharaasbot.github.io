About

Making shop automation practical.

Pharaas exists to make it possible to operate a retail shop through robotics and software while giving the owner remote control of the business.

Why Pharaas exists

A shop has many tasks that can be automated.

A customer needs to find a product, place an order, pay, receive the product, and have the transaction recorded.

The shop needs to manage inventory and remain operational throughout the process.

Pharaas brings these activities together into an automated retail workflow.

What Pharaas is

A robotic system for operating a shop.

Pharaas combines physical robotics with remote-operations software installed into a retail outlet.

The physical system handles products. The software connects customer interactions, payments, inventory, transactions, and remote management.

The result is an automated shop that the owner can operate and monitor remotely.

Design principles

How the product is designed.

The shop comes first

Every retail business has its own products and operating requirements. Pharaas is designed around the outlet and the way it needs to trade.

Remote operation is central

The owner should have visibility into the business without needing to remain at the outlet. Remote operations are central to the product.

The whole transaction is covered

Automation has to cover the customer journey. Pharaas connects product selection, payment, fulfilment, inventory, and operational information.

One platform, many kinds of retail

The same core platform can be configured for different types of shops, including general retail, electronics, hardware, spares, and pharmacy.

Product philosophy

The technology should fit the retail business.

Pharaas starts with the shop and the way customers buy. The system is designed to automate the physical work that can be reliably automated while giving the owner useful information and control over the operation.
